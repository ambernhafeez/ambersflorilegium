## About Florilegium Press

If you listen closely, singular voices are whispering forgotten stories... 

Florilegium Press is an independent audiobook publisher specialising in overlooked public domain works. 

Through Author's Republic, Florilegium Press audiobooks are distrubuted to all major platforms and to libraries.
